The model

    
Battery 12 V; diodes D1/D2 drop 0.8 V; the L298N drops 1.8 V; pump winding 2 Ω, 0.8 A running at full speed, load rising with the square of its speed. The divider is set so A0 sees 0.300 of the RES voltage, as kRatioPermil = 300 assumes. Readings jitter by ±1 count.
